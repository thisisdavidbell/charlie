Marble It Up

marble it up is a fun game for nintendo switch, ps5, ps4, xbox series x, xbox one, and pc. its very enjoyable navagating your marble through the levels including the easy learning to roll to the legendery stratosphere. lets look at some of the features!

PLATFORMS ( different colors ) : Smooth, solid, hard surfaces that offer normal traction and predictable physics for rolling and high-speed maneuvers.

MOVING PLATFORMS( red, blue, or purple ): travel horizontally, vertically, or in loops. to stay on them you need to constantly roll against their motion. red ones move on their own, while blue ones only move when you are on them. the purple ones turn rather than move.

SPRING PLATFORMS ( yellow ) : bounce off of them to reach higher areas.

GRAVITY PLATFORMS ( black ) : these platforms change the direction of gravity when you roll over them, turning the walls and ceilings into the floor, allowing you to walk on walls and ceilings.

ice PLATFORMS ( bluey white ) : these platforms are slippery and will make you slide around, making it harder to control your marble.

bashers ( red cubes with a white angry face ) : these cubes are designed to bash into your marble, knocking it into the oblivion.

thin PLATFORMS ( gray ) : these platforms are very thin and will make it harder to control your marble, as you can easily fall off of them.

thin platform patrollers ( grey with an orange ring ) : these are moving cylinders that patrol along thin platforms if you get hit by one it will knock you off.

cradle (normal platform swinging from a metal bar) this platform swings back and forth, and you need to time your jumps to get on it.

cloud (white) these block your view of the level, making it harder to see where you are going. they move out of the way when you roll into them.

crusher (2 gray gears) these are 2 gears that crush your marble if you get caught between them.

bumper (purple with a grey top) these are obticles that will bounce your marble away if you roll into them. they can be used to give you a little jump boost.

skin/trail unlocker (instant)(clear box with a disco ball inside) these are collectible items that unlock new skins and trails for your marble.

super speed (yellow lightning bolt) these powerups give your marble a speed boost, allowing you to travel faster through the levels.but how fast is too fast?

super jump (green up arrow) these powerups give your marble a jump boost, allowing you to reach higher areas.

feather (white feather) these powerups reduce the gravity on your marble, making it easier to cross long gaps.

time slower (instant)(grey pause symbol) these powerups slow down the game clock, giving you more time to complete levels. giving you faster times
